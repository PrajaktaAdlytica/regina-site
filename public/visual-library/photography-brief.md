# Regina — photography brief

## Direction

Real Polish environments, documentary activity, human agency. Warm-neutral grading, natural light, credible skin tones. Capture environmental views, people working and practical details. Prefer collaboration over posed handshakes.

Avoid futuristic AI cityscapes, holograms, neon brains and fictional laboratories. Conceptual generated illustrations must remain separate from documentary photography.

## Priority shot list

1. Warsaw as a contemporary working city: offices, streets and civic spaces. Misja/Kontakt context only, not an implied Regina office.
2. Entrepreneurs and small teams reviewing practical plans. Home/Partnerstwo. Prefer actual Regina events or cleared partner settings.
3. Students/trainers working with equipment at a verified Polish university or vocational institution. Działania/Edukacja.
4. Laboratory researchers using instruments. Działania/Badania. Verify institution, location and permissions.
5. Engineers, modern production and quality control. Działania/Innowacje. No unrelated manufacturer implied as a client.
6. A public-administration workshop or consultation. Partnerstwo, supporting imagery only; no implied public endorsement.
7. People of different ages learning/using digital tools. Home/Działania. People before devices.
8. Actual project activity and participants. Projekty. More valuable than generic stock.

## Production

Keep 3:2 masters; prepare 4:3 or square card crops. Seek natural negative space beside subjects. Retain safety equipment and documentary meaning in crops. Prepare responsive AVIF/WebP and JPEG fallback during implementation. Avoid blanket burgundy or monochrome grading. Alt text must describe visible activity without unverified affiliation or impact.

## Sourcing and rights

First choice: foundation-owned or partner-supplied images with documented permission. Otherwise verified Polish contextual stock. A public press gallery is not automatically reusable. Search results alone do not verify location or rights.

City discovery starting point: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/warsaw-poland . This is not a cleared image selection. No photograph has been approved or downloaded for this library.

For each candidate record: stable asset ID; photographer and attribution; exact original image page; licence URL and date; permitted uses/restrictions; verified location and evidence; any required model/property/institutional releases; actual project versus contextual stock; page/crop; factual caption and alt; approval status (cleared/pending/reference-only).

Leave a slot empty or use the vector system if provenance/rights are not resolved. Do not replace real project photography with fictional AI-generated evidence.
